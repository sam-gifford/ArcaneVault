Background
Arcane Vault is a startup company aiming to build an online platform that connects local collectible
hobby shops and collectors, allowing them to buy, sell, and trade collectible items through a
centralized marketplace.

Before investing in a full-scale marketplace system, the company has decided to first develop a
prototype web application focused on collection management. The prototype will allow users,
consisting of shop owners and collectors, to manage their personal collections online while enabling
the company to gather valuable insights into:

•  Popular collectible items and collections

•  User activity and engagement

•  Market demand and collection trends

•  Overall interest within the collector community

In addition, website staff (administrators) will be able to manage the master database of collection
categories available on the platform.

The success of this prototype will help Arcane Vault evaluate the feasibility and future direction of
the planned marketplace platform.

Minimum Business Requirements
Based on discussions with Arcane Vault, the system will support at least two main user roles, each
with a defined set of functions:

1. Users (Shop Owners and Collectors)
Role
The User role, assumed by shop owners and collectors, can use the website to manage their
personal collectible collections.

Functional Requirements
The User shall be able to:

•  Register for a User account

•

Log in and log out securely

•  Add items to their personal collection

•  View items in their collection

•  Update collection records

•  Remove items from their collection

2. Staff (Administrator)
Role
The Staff role, assumed by Arcane Vault’s system administrator, is responsible for managing the
platform’s master data to ensure accurate categorization of collection items. This includes defining
and maintaining a clear and consistent list of collection categories that the website supports,
enabling users to properly organize and classify their collections.

In addition, the Staff role has access to all functionalities available to the User role.

Functional Requirements
The Staff accounts shall be able to:

•  Create new collection categories

•  View category details

•  Update category information

•  Delete categories

System requirements
You are required to design and develop the prototype system using the following ASP.NET Core
technologies introduced in this learning unit:

•  Web Application Layer

-  ASP.NET Core Razor Pages Web Application
